Since this website is the pet project of a full-time physician who does not yet have the resources or time to manage account password authentication logistics, she has opted to outsource the authentication component to Google and Yahoo for now. (No judgement — we all forget our passwords sometimes.) Basically, we're just using Google and Yahoo for game account creation so that you can save your game progress, but we never see your password, contacts, etc. Details below.

Third-Party Authentication & Account Creation

When you log into our game using a third-party service such as Google or Yahoo, we utilize open authorization (OAuth) protocols. This allows you to create and access your account securely. Your third-party password is never shared with us.

Information We Collect

Unique Account Identifier: An anonymized ID provided by Google or Yahoo to save your game progress and log you in.

Email Address: Used solely for backend account identification, support, and account recovery. It is never displayed publicly or shared with other players.

In-Game Identity & Privacy

Custom Usernames & Avatars: Your in-game profile relies entirely on the custom username and avatar you select on our site.

No Public Link: Your real name, Google/Yahoo display name, and third-party profile pictures are never displayed in the game or visible to other players.

Information We NEVER Access or Store

  • Your Google or Yahoo passwords.
  • Your contacts, address book, or social connections.
  • Your emails, personal messages, or search history.
  • Permission to post, message, or perform actions on your behalf.
