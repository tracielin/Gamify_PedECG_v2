What are lives?

You get 4 lives to complete this level. You lose a "life" for each wrong answer on a multiple choice question. Wrong answers on free-text questions don't cost any lives; you just won't progress towards level completion. If you lose all 4 lives, you'll get the chance to retry the level from the beginning or do a different learning method before coming back to try the level again.

What are METs?

In this game, METs are your Motivation/Endurance/Tenacity points. The more questions or challenges you face, the more METs you gain. Think of them like exercise METs (metabolic equivalent of task): 3 or less is like light exercise, 4 to 6 is moderate intensity exercise, and 7 or more is a vigorous intensity workout.
